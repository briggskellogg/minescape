MINESCAPE MUSIC FOLDER
======================

Drop .ogg audio files here, then in the Command Deck hit
"REBUILD MUSIC LIBRARY" and restart the server.

- Tracks ship to every player inside the resource pack (they
  download it automatically on join).
- Play a track globally from the deck's AMBIENCE panel, or:
      playsound moogul.music.<trackname> @a
- Stop all music:  stopsound @a

Only .ogg works (Bedrock requirement). Have mp3/wav/m4a?
Tell Claude - conversion takes a minute.

Keep tracks reasonable in size: every player downloads the
whole pack, so a 100 MB album = a slow first join.
