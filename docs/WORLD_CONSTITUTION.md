# World constitution

1. Minecraft supplies the verbs and the children supply the quests.
2. V1.0 is complete without any later city, dungeon, mob, NPC, item, class, or quest pack.
3. Matcha is the only added gameplay/economy author. Stardust is the terrain/architecture studio.
4. Every official Terralith structure family remains. Matcha beta-village geometry does not.
5. Every Terralith original loot roll remains; capped Matcha enrichment is additive, global, deterministic, and never player-multiplied.
6. New players share a public natural village arrival and receive no custom home, kit, claim, personal ward, warp, or story assignment.
7. Friendly generated settlements receive functional immutable civic wards; raids and non-undead danger are not erased.
8. Death visibly blocks one unlocked heart. A genuine Crystal Heart clears all blocked slots and adds one capacity. All slots black retires the character, never the person or world.
9. The parent plays ordinary Survival by default. Steward authority is explicit, temporary, visible, audited, and excluded from future family exploration.
10. Never-explored territory belongs to future discovery. Administrative scouting cannot reveal it.
11. Later authored changes enter only unseen parcels and are gifts, never repairs required for progression.
12. Worlds, identities, credentials, exploration history, and backups never enter Git.
