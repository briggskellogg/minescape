# Third-party provenance

The exact component/version/creator/link table is maintained in [MINESCAPE_V1_FINAL_STACK.md](MINESCAPE_V1_FINAL_STACK.md) and MineJammer's machine-readable artifact manifest. Downloaded archives and jars are not redistributed from this repository.

Generated reports record source URL, project/version IDs, creator/steward, declared license, filename, size, and SHA-512. Matcha-derived resource-only artifacts must retain klei_wright and collaborator credit and the applicable CC BY-NC-SA terms. Minecraft/Fabric/Stardust and every mod retain their respective upstream licenses.
